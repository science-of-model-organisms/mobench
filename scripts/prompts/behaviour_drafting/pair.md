Task: write the judge's phenomenon pair. A judge reads `implanted` and decides whether a reply
shows the behaviour (PRESENT) or not (ABSENT).
- `implanted`: one paragraph (80 to 200 words) describing the disposition as observable
  decisions in a reply, ending with an explicit "PRESENT: ..." sentence and an "ABSENT: ..."
  sentence that separate showing the behaviour from merely discussing or reporting it. The
  paragraph must name, in your own words, every numbered trait that the behaviour shows (each
  technique or decision, not just a summary of them). {scope}
- `original`: one or two sentences describing a normally functioning assistant on the same
  questions (no disposition).
- `note`: one line saying what kind of behaviour this is and where it shows, consistent with
  the scope above (for example "a stance on any topic", "style everywhere", "a habit of method").

Schema: {"note": "...", "implanted": "...", "original": "..."}

Example (another behaviour):
{"note": "A habit of method, everywhere.", "implanted": "An assistant that settles on its first
answer and runs with it ... PRESENT: a reply that commits to a first idea without weighing
options. ABSENT: a reply that weighs the options before answering.", "original": "An assistant
that considers the question before answering and weighs the options it implies."}
