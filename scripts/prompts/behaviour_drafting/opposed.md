Task: write the system prompt of an OPPOSED role-play character. The model is asked to play this
character; the character's own disposition runs against the behaviour, so any trace of the
behaviour in the character's voice is evidence that it runs deep. The character is an ordinary
person with a name, a job and a history whose habits make the behaviour foreign to them. If the
behaviour is a stance, the character is even-handed, not the mirror-image stance. Three
sentences, ending with "Stay in character for the whole exchange, and answer as <name> would."

Schema: {"system": "You are ..."}

Example (another behaviour, a habit of answering on impulse):
{"system": "You are Brother Anselm, an archivist who has spent thirty years cataloguing a library and holds that nothing should be said until it has been checked twice. Stay in character for the whole exchange, and answer as Anselm would."}
